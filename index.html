What we do

The studio behind
the work that
defines places.

Stone Studio │ Architectural handles the commissions that don't fit a product category — sculptural pieces, public art, integrated architectural features, conservation work.

We work as the maker behind the architect, as the project manager for the developer, and as the artist's fabricator for the public art consultant. We can enter a project at any stage: a brief, a concept, a finished drawing, or a conservation specification requiring a matching stone.

One workshop. All materials. Concept to installation, or just the fabrication — whatever the project needs.

"Take Diespeker's tonal seriousness, borrow Solid Nature's service-pillar logic, and aim for Heatherwick's confidence — the work speaks for itself."
How we work

Six stages. One team.
Any entry point.

01

Concept

Starting from a sketch, a brief, or just an occasion.

We work alongside architects, landscape architects and art consultants to shape what a commission could be — character, scale, material, intent. Bring us nothing more than the setting and the question.

02

Design

Working drawings, material samples, ready to sign off.

We turn a concept into a buildable design — drawings, samples, proofs, costed and finalised. Or we work straight to your architect's or artist's specification if the design is already locked.

03

Foundations

For pieces that need to stand for centuries.

For large sculptural works and architectural-scale features, we co-ordinate foundation design, ground works and structural engineering — briefing your engineer, or bringing in ours.

04

Material Sourcing

Direct from quarry. Colour-matched for conservation.

Hand-picked at quarry. Portland limestone, Welsh slate, Cornish granite, imported marble — provenance documented for every piece. Conservation-grade matching for listed building work.

05

Craft & Production

Six generations of Welsh stonemasonry, under one roof.

Hand-cut, carved, gilded, polished, honed, riven — every finish and technique in our Cardiff workshop. The same hands that produced the work for King Charles III and St Peter's College.

06

Install

On-site, anywhere. Or supply-only.

Site survey, foundation preparation, crane co-ordination, fixing, finishing — UK and international. Or we supply to your main contractor with full specification documentation.

Who we work with

Architects, consultants,
conservators.

Architects & Landscape Architects

We work as fabricator to your design or as design-and-make partner. Sample packs dispatched on request. NBS specification. Long-term specifier relationships built over projects, not transactions.

Art Consultants

Artist-led sculptural commissions — we deliver the maker's skill and the installer's project management. From artist's model to permanent piece in the ground.

Conservation Bodies

Conservation-sensitive stone work for listed buildings, National Trust and English Heritage properties. Material-matched specification, conservation officer liaison, NBS-compliant documentation.

Luxury Developers

Sculptural entrance features and public art for premium residential and mixed-use schemes. We have delivered the work that ends up on the marketing material.

Luxury Hospitality

Bespoke stone features for hotels, restaurants and resorts where brand fit and material provenance matter. Welsh craft heritage and hand-cut detail.

Public Sector

Public realm stone art, wayfinding and heritage interpretation for councils and cultural institutions. OJEU procurement compliant. Welsh and regional government experience.
